## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website installs on your browser, allowing it, among other things, to store and retrieve information about your browsing habits. This policy also covers similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage.

## Cookies used on this website

This website **does not use advertising or profiling cookies**. Only the following may be installed:

| Cookie | Owner | Type | Purpose | Duration | Consent required? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google (third party) | Analytics | Distinguish visitors anonymously to produce aggregated usage statistics. | 2 years | Yes |
| `_ga_WG0JW6FWEC` | Google (third party) | Analytics | Keep the state of the browsing session. | 2 years | Yes |
| `NID`, `AEC`, `SOCS` and similar | Google (third party) | Preferences and security (map) | Show the Google Maps map and keep its preferences and security. Set by Google when the map loads. | Up to 13 months, according to Google | Yes |
| `doria-cookies` (local storage) | Doria Clinic (first party) | Technical | Remember your choice about cookies. | 12 months | No, strictly necessary |

- **Google Analytics cookies.** We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the website is used (most visited pages, where visits come from) so we can improve it. **They are only installed if you accept them** in the cookie notice; until you do, no Google Analytics resource is loaded. The data is processed by Google Ireland Ltd. and may involve a transfer to the United States, covered by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). More information in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Web hosting.** The site is served from Netlify, which does not install any cookie in your browser when you visit this website.
- **Google Maps map.** The map in the footer is provided by Google Maps. **It is not loaded until you accept cookies** in the notice; if you have not accepted them you will see a box with a "Show map" button, which loads the map only at that moment and by your express choice, and an "Open in Google Maps" link that takes you to Google's site. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and receives data such as your IP address; [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.
- **Social networks and contact buttons.** Links to Instagram and other networks, and the WhatsApp, call and email buttons, simply take you to the relevant service or open the app on your device; they do not load third-party content on our website or install cookies.

## How to manage your consent

- The first time you visit the website you will see a notice where you can **accept or reject** analytics and map cookies with equal ease. You can keep browsing without accepting them.
- You can **change or withdraw your choice at any time** using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of every page. If you reject them after having accepted, we delete the Google Analytics cookies and remove the map from the page; cookies Google may have set when loading the map can only be deleted from your browser settings.
- We keep your choice for 12 months; after that we will ask you again.
- You can also allow, block or delete cookies in your browser settings (see its help menu).
