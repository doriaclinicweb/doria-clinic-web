## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website installs on your browser, allowing it, among other things, to store and retrieve information about your browsing habits.

## Cookies used on this website

This website does not use advertising cookies. The cookies that may be installed are:

- **Cloudflare technical cookies**, from our hosting provider, necessary to serve the website securely and to balance load between servers. They are not used for advertising purposes and do not require your consent.
- **Google Analytics cookies**: we use Google Analytics to understand, in an aggregated and anonymous way, how the website is used (most visited pages, where visits come from) so we can improve it. These cookies are only installed if you accept them in the cookie notice. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.
- **Google Maps cookies**: on the site's pages we embed a Google map to show the clinic's location. When you interact with the map, Google may set its own cookies, subject to [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

None of this website's contact buttons (WhatsApp, call, email) install cookies: they simply open the corresponding app on your device.

## How to manage your consent

The first time you visit the website you can accept or reject analytics cookies from the notice shown on screen. If you reject them, no Google Analytics cookie will be installed. You can also allow, block or delete cookies installed on your device through your browser's settings; please note that blocking Google Maps cookies may prevent the map from displaying correctly.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page if we introduce new tools or services that use cookies in the future.
