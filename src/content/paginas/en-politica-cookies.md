## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website installs on your browser, allowing it, among other things, to store and retrieve information about your browsing habits.

## Cookies used on this website

This is a static website: it has no analytics system of its own and no advertising cookies. The only cookies that may be installed are:

- **Cloudflare technical cookies**, from our hosting provider, necessary to serve the website securely and to balance load between servers. They are not used for advertising purposes.
- **Google Maps cookies**: on the site's pages we embed a Google map to show the clinic's location. When you interact with the map, Google may set its own cookies, subject to [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

None of this website's contact buttons (WhatsApp, call, email) install cookies: they simply open the corresponding app on your device.

## How to disable cookies

You can allow, block or delete cookies installed on your device through your browser's settings. Please note that blocking Google Maps cookies may prevent the map from displaying correctly.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page if we introduce analytics tools or other services that use cookies in the future.
