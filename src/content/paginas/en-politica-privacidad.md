## 2. How we handle your data on this website

This is a static, informational website: it does not include its own data collection forms or a patient database. When you contact us through the WhatsApp, call or email buttons shown on the site, the communication takes place directly between you and Doria Clinic through the application or service you choose, and no data is recorded on this website.

## 3. Patient data

Health data you provide when booking an appointment or during your visit to the clinic is processed in accordance with data protection regulations and applicable healthcare regulations (including Spanish Law 41/2002, on patient autonomy), and is managed separately from this website, within the clinic's own systems.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis

When you write to us by WhatsApp, email or phone, we process the data you provide (name, phone number, reason for enquiry) in order to respond to your request and, where applicable, manage your appointment. The legal basis for this processing is your own consent, given by initiating contact yourself.

## 5. Recipients

We do not share your data with third parties, except where legally required. If you write to us via WhatsApp, please note that Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., as the service provider, processes the communication's metadata in accordance with its own privacy policy.

## 6. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to [info@doria.clinic](mailto:info@doria.clinic) or by post to Carrer Villarroel, 221, 08036 Barcelona, enclosing a copy of a document proving your identity. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) if you believe we have not properly handled your request.

## 7. Data retention

We only keep the data from the communications you send us for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry, unless the relationship leads to clinical care, in which case the retention periods for medical records set by healthcare legislation apply.

## 8. Cookies

This website uses first-party and third-party cookies. You can find the details in our [cookie policy](/en/politica-cookies/).

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect legislative developments or changes to the website. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
