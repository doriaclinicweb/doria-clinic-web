## What it is

Temporomandibular joint dysfunction (TMJ) and bruxism (clenching or grinding your
teeth) are two of the most common causes of jaw pain, headaches and dental wear.
They often go hand in hand: the tension from bruxism overloads the joint that
connects the jaw to the skull.

## How it's treated

The most common solution is a custom night guard, made with a digital scan to fit
your bite precisely. It's worn while you sleep and distributes bite force evenly,
protecting the enamel from wear and relaxing the jaw muscles.

## When to come in

If you notice pain on waking, clicking when opening your mouth, or your partner
mentions you grind your teeth at night, it's worth having a check-up within
[general dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-general/). The sooner it's
brought under control, the less wear builds up on your teeth. It's related to
[sports dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-deportiva/), where bite balance
also affects physical performance.
