## What it is

The standard protocol for [dental implants](/en/especialidades/implantes-dentales/)
requires a bone integration period of about two months before placing the final
tooth. Immediate loading shortens that wait by placing a fixed provisional
prosthesis on the same day as the surgery.

## What to expect

You leave the clinic with fixed teeth. The provisional prosthesis is replaced with
the final one once the implant has finished integrating into the bone.

## Care for the first few days

- **Soft diet**: during the first week, to avoid putting strain on the provisional
  teeth while the area heals.
- **Gentle hygiene**: careful brushing around the implants, reinforced with
  mouthwash if we recommend it, to keep the area clean without irritating it.
- **No intense physical activity**: avoid high-impact exercise for the first 48
  hours to avoid encouraging inflammation.
- **Scheduled check-ups**: we monitor the implant's integration until the final
  prosthesis is placed.

If there isn't enough bone to give the implant primary stability, we first consider
[bone regeneration](/en/especialidades/cirugia-oral/) as part of the same initial
study.
