## What it is

A prosthesis on natural teeth is supported by your own natural teeth, rather than
implants, within our [dental prosthetics](/en/especialidades/protesis-dental/)
area. It can be fixed (a crown or bridge, cemented in) or removable (the patient
puts it in and takes it out).

## When it's recommended

It's a good option when the teeth neighbouring the area to be replaced are healthy
and can act as support, offering a faster and, in many cases, more affordable
solution than a [prosthesis on implants](/en/tratamientos/protesis-sobre-implantes/).
