## What it is

When root canal treatment or a retreatment fail to clear the infection at the tip
of the root, apical microsurgery makes it possible to access the source directly
from outside, clean it and seal the root from the tip.

It's the last resort in conservative dentistry before considering
[extraction](/en/especialidades/cirugia-oral/).

## Care after the procedure

- **Local cold**: applying cold to the cheek in the first few hours helps control
  inflammation.
- **Soft, cold diet** on the first day, avoiding chewing on the treated side.
- **Normal hygiene**, avoiding brushing directly over the incision site for the
  first few days.
- **Follow-up check-up**: we assess healing with an X-ray after a few months to
  confirm the bone has regenerated correctly.
