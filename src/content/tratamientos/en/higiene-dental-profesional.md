## What it is

Professional dental hygiene removes bacterial plaque and tartar built up in areas
daily brushing doesn't reach, especially between teeth and beneath the gumline,
within [general dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-general/).

## Why it matters

It's the most effective preventive treatment against decay and
[gum disease](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/). Catching gum inflammation early,
at the gingivitis stage, stops it progressing to periodontitis, which is far
harder to reverse.
