## What it is

A prosthesis on implants is anchored onto one or more
[dental implants](/en/especialidades/implantes-dentales/) already integrated into
the bone, rather than resting on natural teeth, within our
[dental prosthetics](/en/especialidades/protesis-dental/) area.

## Advantages over a prosthesis on natural teeth

It doesn't require wearing down any natural tooth to act as support, and the
anchor in the bone offers a stability and bite feel very similar to your own
tooth, especially valuable in
[full-arch](/en/tratamientos/implantes-arcada-completa/) restorations.
