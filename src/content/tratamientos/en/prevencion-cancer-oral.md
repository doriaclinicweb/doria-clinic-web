## What it is

The oral cancer screening is a visual and physical examination of the entire oral
mucosa — lips, tongue, gums, palate and floor of the mouth — to catch any
suspicious lesion at an early stage, when treatment is simpler and more effective.

## Why it matters

Because it develops inside the mouth, oral cancer can go unnoticed for months. Many
lesions don't hurt in their early stages. Early detection is the factor that most
influences prognosis, which is why we include it as part of
[general dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-general/) at every routine
check-up.

## Risk factors

Tobacco and alcohol are the main risk factors, especially in combination.
Prolonged sun exposure on the lips and certain infections also play a role. If you
belong to a higher-risk group, we'll let you know so we can increase the frequency
of your check-ups.
