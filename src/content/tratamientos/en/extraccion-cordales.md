## What it is

Wisdom tooth extraction removes wisdom teeth when they don't have space to come
through properly or are causing problems, within our
[oral surgery](/en/especialidades/cirugia-oral/) area.

## When it's recommended

Wisdom teeth usually appear between the ages of 17 and 25 and often don't have
enough space to come through properly. Extraction is recommended when there's a
lack of space, impacted teeth, repeated infections (pericoronitis) or a risk of
decay due to poor access for cleaning.

## Aftercare

For the first few days, a soft, cold diet, avoiding vigorous rinsing and intense
physical activity, and following the prescribed medication to control inflammation
and prevent infection.
