## What it is

Teeth whitening lightens the natural colour of the tooth using a peroxide-based
gel, applied under professional supervision within our
[dental aesthetics](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) area.

## Methods

- **In clinic (flash session)**: high-concentration gel activated with
  professional light, with visible results in around an hour.
- **At home**: custom trays and professional gel to do it at your own pace from
  home.
- **Combined**: the in-clinic session together with at-home reinforcement, the
  longest-lasting option.

## Aftercare

For the first 2-3 days avoid food and drink with strong pigments (coffee, red
wine, tomato sauce) and tobacco, since the enamel is more porous right after
whitening and absorbs colour more easily.
