## What it is

Periodontal microsurgery applies the principles of minimally invasive surgery to
gum treatment: instead of conventional instruments, we use a microscope or
high-power loupes, micro-instruments and very fine sutures.

## Advantages over conventional surgery

The incisions are much smaller, which reduces inflammation, bleeding and
post-operative discomfort, as well as improving the final aesthetic result. It's
the technique we use whenever the case allows within our
[periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) area, especially for
[gum grafts](/en/tratamientos/injerto-de-encia/).
