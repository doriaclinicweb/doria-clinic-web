## What it is

Gum pigmentation is the build-up of melanin in the gums, giving them a dark or
uneven tone. It's entirely benign, but it can affect the appearance of the smile
when a lot of gum shows while speaking or smiling. Depigmentation safely removes
that surface layer.

## How it's done

Depending on the case, we use laser or a minimally invasive surgical technique to
remove the pigmented layer of the gum. It's an outpatient procedure, under local
anaesthesia, within our [periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) area.

## Aftercare

For the first few days it's normal for the gum to feel more sensitive; avoid very
hot, spicy or acidic food and keep up gentle hygiene with a soft brush until it's
fully healed. It's a common treatment within a broader
[dental aesthetics](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) plan.
