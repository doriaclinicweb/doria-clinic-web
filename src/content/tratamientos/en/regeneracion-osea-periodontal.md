## What it is

When periodontitis destroys the bone that supports a tooth, periodontal bone
regeneration uses grafts and, in many cases, a biocompatible membrane to guide the
growth of new bone in the affected area, within our
[periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) area.

## How it differs from regeneration before an implant

Although the technique is similar to the one we use before placing a
[dental implant](/en/especialidades/implantes-dentales/) in
[oral surgery](/en/especialidades/cirugia-oral/), the goal here is different: saving
a natural tooth you already have, not preparing an area for a future implant.

## A prior requirement

Regenerating bone only makes sense once the periodontal infection is under control.
That's why we always start by treating the periodontitis before considering
regeneration.
