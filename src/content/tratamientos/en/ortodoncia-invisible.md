## What it is

Invisible orthodontics uses custom-made clear aligners that move teeth gradually,
within our [orthodontics](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/) area. It's the most
popular option among adults and teenagers looking for discretion and comfort.

## Advantages over braces

They can be removed to eat and brush your teeth, are practically unnoticeable and,
since they have no wires, avoid rubbing and mouth sores. For more complex bite
issues, we assess whether [braces](/en/tratamientos/brackets-dentales/) are a
more suitable option.
