## What it is

Veneers are thin layers of porcelain or composite bonded to the visible face of
the tooth to correct its colour, shape or minor malpositions, within our
[dental aesthetics](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) area.

## Before veneers

We design your smile virtually with digital planning so you can see the final
result before starting. If we detect decay, inflamed gums or very misaligned
teeth, we resolve that first in
[general dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-general/),
[periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) or
[orthodontics](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/).
