## What it is

Crown lengthening is a periodontal surgical technique that exposes more healthy
tooth structure, reshaping the gum and, if needed, the underlying bone, within
[periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/).

## When it's indicated

It's necessary when a tooth is heavily worn or fractured and there isn't enough
visible structure to fit a [crown or prosthesis](/en/especialidades/protesis-dental/)
stably, or when the gum covers too much of the tooth (a "gummy smile") and a more
harmonious result is wanted as part of a
[dental aesthetics](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) plan.
