## What it is

Braces are the most precise tool in [orthodontics](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/)
for correcting complex bite problems, using small brackets fixed to each tooth
that, connected by an archwire, guide their gradual movement.

## Types of braces

- **Traditional metal**: the most common and durable, with ligatures that hold the
  archwire.
- **Aesthetic (sapphire or ceramic)**: work the same as metal braces but blend in
  with the tooth's colour.
- **Self-ligating**: don't need elastics; a system of clips reduces friction, which
  can speed up treatment and space out visits.

If you're looking for a removable, practically invisible alternative, also see
[invisible orthodontics](/en/tratamientos/ortodoncia-invisible/).
