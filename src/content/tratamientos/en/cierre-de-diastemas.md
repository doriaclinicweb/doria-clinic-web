## What it is

A diastema is a visible gap between two teeth, most often between the upper
central incisors. It can be due to a mismatch between tooth size and jaw size, a
prominent [lip frenum](/en/especialidades/cirugia-oral/), or the loss of a
neighbouring tooth.

## How it's closed

The approach depends on the cause and the size of the gap. When the gap is wide or
several teeth are poorly positioned, the most stable solution is
[orthodontics](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/), which moves the teeth to their
ideal position. For small gaps with teeth that are already aligned, a
[veneer](/en/tratamientos/carillas-dentales/) or a composite reconstruction within
[dental aesthetics](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) can close the gap in a
single visit.
