## What it is

A single-tooth implant replaces the root of a single missing tooth, providing the
base for the final crown that will do its job, within our
[dental implants](/en/especialidades/implantes-dentales/) area.

## Why replace a missing tooth

Beyond the aesthetic improvement, replacing the tooth is key to keeping the
mouth's structure intact: if it isn't replaced, the neighbouring teeth start
shifting to fill the empty space, complicating chewing and future treatment. If
you'd rather not wait the two months for bone integration, ask about the
[immediate-loading](/en/tratamientos/carga-inmediata/) protocol.
