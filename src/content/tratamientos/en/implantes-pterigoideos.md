## What it is

The pterygoid implant is anchored in the pterygoid bone, a dense, stable area at
the back of the palate that's rarely used in conventional implantology. It's an
alternative to a sinus lift or bone graft when there isn't enough bone at the back
of the upper [jaw](/en/especialidades/implantes-dentales/).

## When it's used

It's mainly considered for patients with advanced bone loss in the upper jaw who
want a fixed-teeth solution without undergoing prior bone regeneration surgery
within [oral surgery](/en/especialidades/cirugia-oral/). It's often combined with
conventional implants at the front to support a
[full-arch prosthesis](/en/tratamientos/implantes-arcada-completa/).
