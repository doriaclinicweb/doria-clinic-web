## What it is

A mouthguard made from a digital scan of your mouth, with thickness and number of
layers adjusted to your sport, within our
[sports dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-deportiva/) area.

Because it fits precisely, it lets you speak and breathe normally during exertion,
which is exactly where standard mouthguards fail.

## Care and maintenance

- **Wash the mouthguard with cold water** and mild soap after each use; hot water
  can warp the material.
- **Always store it in its ventilated case**, never loose in your sports bag or in
  a closed, damp place.
- **Check it periodically**: if you notice it losing its fit, or it's worn or
  cracked, it loses some of its ability to absorb impact.
- If you take a blow to the mouth even while wearing your mouthguard, follow our
  [recommendations for dental trauma](/en/especialidades/traumatologia-dental/).
