## What it is

A gum graft covers the exposed root of a tooth caused by gum recession, using the
patient's own tissue, with minimally invasive techniques and local anaesthesia,
within our [periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) area.

## Why it's recommended

Beyond sensitivity and cosmetic wear, an untreated gum recession can keep
progressing and compromise the tooth's support in the long term. When the case
allows, we carry out the graft using
[periodontal microsurgery](/en/tratamientos/microcirugia-periodontal/) for a more
precise result and a more comfortable recovery.
