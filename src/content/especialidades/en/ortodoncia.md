## What orthodontics is

Orthodontics goes beyond achieving a nice-looking smile: it corrects dental
alignment so the mouth works properly and is easier to clean. By putting each tooth
in its ideal position on the bone structure, we improve
[aesthetics](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) and create a more harmonious
relationship between the upper and lower arches. Aligned teeth accumulate less
tartar and help prevent dental disease.

## [Invisible orthodontics](/en/tratamientos/ortodoncia-invisible/)

Custom-made clear aligners that move teeth gradually. It's the most popular option
among adults and teenagers looking for discretion and comfort: they can be removed
to eat and brush your teeth, they're practically unnoticeable and, since they have
no wires, they avoid rubbing and mouth sores.

## [Braces](/en/tratamientos/brackets-dentales/)

Still the most precise tool for correcting complex bite problems.

- **Traditional metal**: the most common and durable, with ligatures that hold the
  archwire.
- **Aesthetic (sapphire or ceramic)**: work the same as metal braces but blend in
  with the tooth's colour.
- **Self-ligating**: don't need elastics; a system of clips reduces friction, which
  can speed up treatment and space out visits.

## Children's orthodontics

The recommended first visit to the orthodontist is between ages 6 and 7. At that
age, even though baby teeth are still present, it's possible to guide the growth of
the facial bones and ensure the permanent teeth have enough space. Acting early
simplifies future treatment and helps prevent breathing and chewing problems.

- **Interceptive orthodontics**: guides bone growth while the child is still
  developing, with removable or fixed appliances.
- **Corrective orthodontics**: once the permanent teeth have come through, aligns
  them for optimal aesthetics and function.

If you'd like to know more about check-ups and prevention for young children, visit
[paediatric dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontopediatria/).

## Closing gaps between teeth

When the goal is to close a gap (diastema) rather than correct the whole bite, we
assess whether [closing diastemas](/en/tratamientos/cierre-de-diastemas/) with
orthodontics is the most suitable option compared with a quicker cosmetic solution.
