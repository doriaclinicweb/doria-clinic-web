At Doria Clinic we want a visit to the dentist to be a positive, fun experience.
Oral health in childhood is the foundation of a healthy smile in adulthood, so we
focus on prevention and on building a trusting relationship with the youngest
patients.

## Children's check-ups

We don't wait for pain before acting. At regular check-ups we monitor the growth of
the jaws and the eruption of baby and permanent teeth, which allows us to catch bite
or spacing problems early. We also teach children, and their parents, proper
brushing techniques.

## Dental sealants

A thin layer of protective resin applied to the fissures of the permanent molars,
which usually come through from age 6. It stops food debris and bacteria getting
trapped in the hardest areas to brush. It's a quick, painless procedure that
requires no anaesthesia.

## Preventive treatments

- **Professional fluoride treatment**: high-concentration fluoride to remineralise
  enamel and make it more resistant to acids.
- **Habit control**: dummy use, thumb-sucking and diet, to prevent future
  malformations.

## Interceptive orthodontics

Carried out while the child still has baby teeth or is in the middle of the
transition. Its goal isn't just to align the teeth, but to correct the bone
structure while it's still mouldable: widening the palate or guiding jaw growth so
the permanent teeth have enough space. Treating a bite misalignment at age 7 or 8
can prevent much longer, more costly treatment during adolescence. More on
[orthodontics](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/).
