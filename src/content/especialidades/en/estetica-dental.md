We see dental aesthetics as the balance between health and harmony, not "off the
shelf" smiles: it's the coordinated planning across every specialty to achieve a
result that brings out your best, naturally.

## Digital smile planning

Before starting any treatment, we design your smile virtually so you can see the
final result, adjust every detail to your facial features and choose textures and
shades that mimic natural enamel.

## [Dental veneers](/en/tratamientos/carillas-dentales/)

Thin layers of high-quality porcelain or composite that blend with your natural
teeth, correcting colour, shape or position in a minimally invasive way.

- **Porcelain**: the longest-lasting option. It doesn't wear down, keeps its shine
  over the years and is highly resistant to staining from coffee, wine or tobacco.
  It perfectly mimics the translucency of enamel.
- **Composite**: ideal for simpler corrections. In most cases it's shaped directly
  on the tooth in a single session, requires almost no preparation of the natural
  tooth and is easy to repair if it's damaged. It needs annual maintenance.

## [Teeth whitening](/en/tratamientos/blanqueamiento-dental/)

- **In clinic (flash session)**: high-concentration gel activated with professional
  light, with results in a session of around an hour.
- **At home**: custom trays and professional gel to do it at your own pace, gradually
  and comfortably.
- **Combined**: the in-clinic session together with at-home reinforcement. It's the
  longest-lasting treatment.

## When aesthetics starts with another specialty

A healthy smile is the foundation of a beautiful one. If, before veneers or
whitening, we detect decay, inflamed gums or misaligned teeth, we resolve that
first with [general dentistry](/en/especialidades/odontologia-general/),
[periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) or
[orthodontics](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/). When teeth are missing or there's
more damaged structure than a veneer can fix, the solution is a
[dental prosthesis](/en/especialidades/protesis-dental/).
