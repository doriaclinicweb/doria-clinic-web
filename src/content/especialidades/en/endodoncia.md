## What root canal treatment is

Endodontics, popularly known as a "root canal", is the treatment that saves a tooth
whose pulp is damaged or infected. Instead of extracting the tooth, we clean and
seal its interior to eliminate pain and let it keep doing its job.

Acting in time avoids intense discomfort and lets you keep your natural smile.
Today it's a quick treatment, usually completed in a single visit, painless and
with a simple recovery.

## Endodontic retreatment

When a previous root canal treatment doesn't heal as expected, retreatment prevents
the loss of the tooth. We start with a thorough study of the causes of the previous
failure and work under magnification, with a microscope or high-power loupes, to
locate missed canals or fissures that are almost invisible to the naked eye.

## Apical microsurgery

When root canal treatment or a retreatment fail to clear the infection at the tip of
the root, apical microsurgery is the last option in conservative dentistry to save
the tooth. [What apical microsurgery involves](/en/tratamientos/microcirugia-apical/).
