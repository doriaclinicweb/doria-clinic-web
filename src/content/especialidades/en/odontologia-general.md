General dentistry is the gateway to your oral health: the check-ups, diagnoses and
basic treatments that keep your mouth healthy and catch any problem in time, before
it becomes more complicated. It's the least glamorous specialty in dentistry and,
precisely because of that, the one that prevents the most problems in the long run.

## When to get a dental check-up

We recommend a check-up every 6 months, even if you don't notice any discomfort. Most
dental conditions — decay, early gingivitis, wear — progress without obvious symptoms
in their early stages. Pain tends to appear once treatment is already more complex and
costly, so a regular check-up is by far the most cost-effective investment in your
oral health.

## What the first visit includes

- **A full examination** of teeth, gums and the soft tissues of the mouth.
- **X-rays**, if the case requires it, to assess what isn't visible to the naked eye.
- **A clear diagnosis and a written treatment plan**, with the available options and
  their cost, so you can decide with full information.
- **No obligation**: the first visit is free and it's up to you whether to go ahead.

## [Professional dental hygiene](/en/tratamientos/higiene-dental-profesional/)

Professional teeth cleaning removes bacterial plaque and tartar built up in areas
daily brushing doesn't reach, especially between teeth and beneath the gumline. It's
the most effective preventive treatment against decay and
[gum disease](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/), and we recommend it once or twice a
year depending on the case.

## Oral cancer screening

Every check-up includes an examination of the lips, tongue, gums and oral mucosa to
catch any suspicious lesion early. More on this
[screening](/en/tratamientos/prevencion-cancer-oral/).

## Fillings

When there's decay, we remove the damaged tissue and rebuild the tooth with
composite that matches the natural tooth's colour, in a conservative and aesthetic
way. The sooner decay is treated, the less of the original tooth needs to be
sacrificed.

## Simple extractions

When a tooth has no viable treatment, we remove it under local anaesthesia with
minimal discomfort, always assessing first whether there's a viable alternative to
save the tooth.

## When we refer you to a specialist

If during a check-up we detect something that needs a more specific approach — for
example, [root canal treatment](/en/especialidades/endodoncia/), a
[receding gum](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) or a
[bite misalignment](/en/especialidades/ortodoncia/) — we'll explain it clearly and
accompany you to the team doctor trained in that area, within the same clinic.

## Jaw pain and bruxism

If we detect dental wear from teeth grinding, or you mention jaw pain on waking, we
consider a [TMJ and bruxism treatment](/en/tratamientos/atm-bruxismo/) with a
custom-made night guard.
