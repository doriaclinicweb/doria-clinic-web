Oral health is a key factor in any athlete's performance. An infection, a poor bite
or gum inflammation can lie behind recurring muscle injuries and slower recovery.
We work with athletes of every level to make their mouth their best ally.

## Custom mouthguards

In contact or impact sports, protection isn't negotiable. We make custom
mouthguards that fit your anatomy, let you speak and breathe normally during
exertion, and absorb and distribute impact to protect your teeth, lips and jaw.
[How they're made](/en/tratamientos/protectores-bucales/).

## Protection by discipline

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all: we adapt the thickness and material to each
sport.

- **Extreme contact** (boxing, MMA, rugby): several layers of impact absorption, to
  protect the teeth and cushion blows affecting the jaw.
- **Team sports** (hockey): reinforced frontal protection against impacts from the
  ball or stick, with a design that allows clear speech.
- **Court sports** (basketball, football): lightness and optimal breathing, against
  accidental elbow knocks or falls.

Mouthguards can be personalised with your team or flag colours, your name, your
number or your club's logo, permanently built in.

## Systemic health and sport

- **Infection sources**: bacteria from the mouth can enter the bloodstream and
  cause inflammation that leads to strains or muscle overload. That's why looking
  after your gums in [periodontics](/en/especialidades/periodoncia/) is also
  looking after your performance.
- **Hygiene and hydration**: isotonic drinks, energy gels and the dry mouth caused
  by exertion increase the risk of decay and erosion. We help you offset this
  without giving up your sports nutrition.
- **Bite balance**: poor alignment can create tension in the temporomandibular
  joint that carries through to the spine, affecting balance and power.

If, despite preventive measures, you suffer a knock while playing sport, see our
[dental trauma](/en/especialidades/traumatologia-dental/) page to know how to act.
