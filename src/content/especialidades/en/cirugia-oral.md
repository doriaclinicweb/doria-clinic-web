The word "surgery" can sound daunting. That's why we combine the highest
technological precision with a warm, personal approach, aiming to resolve oral
conditions permanently while always prioritising a fast recovery.

## Minimally invasive surgery

Thanks to digital planning and 3D X-rays, we map your anatomy down to the
millimetre before we start. This allows much smaller incisions, reduces
post-operative inflammation and pain, and ensures a higher success rate in complex
procedures.

## Wisdom tooth extraction

Wisdom teeth usually appear between the ages of 17 and 25 and often don't have
enough space to come through properly. We carry out their
[extraction](/en/tratamientos/extraccion-cordales/) safely, even when they're
impacted or fully embedded, avoiding displacement of the other teeth and recurring
infections. It's recommended when there's a lack of space, impacted wisdom teeth,
repeated infections (pericoronitis) or a risk of decay due to poor access for
cleaning.

## Implant surgery and bone regeneration

If you've lost bone mass, we apply bone grafts and sinus lifts to provide the
necessary base for your future
[dental implants](/en/especialidades/implantes-dentales/), all digitally planned.
When the case allows, this regeneration is combined with placing
[immediate-loading implants](/en/tratamientos/carga-inmediata/) on the same day.

## Frenectomies and pre-prosthetic surgery

We correct lip or tongue ties that affect speech or tooth position, and prepare the
tissues of the mouth so that
[prostheses](/en/especialidades/protesis-dental/) or
[veneers](/en/especialidades/estetica-dental/) fit in harmoniously.

## Cyst removal and biopsies

Minor procedures to remove lesions in the soft tissues or jawbones, as part of a
comprehensive approach to oral health.
